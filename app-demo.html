2. Purpose & Target Audience

Arena-X Admin is the venue management companion app for sports center owners, managers, and court administrators. It solves the problem of manual scheduling conflicts by allowing venue operators to register their sports center, configure court availability and hourly pricing, monitor real-time bookings made by athletes, and receive instant push notifications.

3. Account Deletion Flow (Guideline 5.1.1(v))

After logging in, tap the Perfil (Profile) tab (4th icon on the bottom navigation bar), scroll down, and tap "Cancelar assinatura / Excluir conta" and confirm in the dialog. The account is deactivated immediately and the user is signed out to the initial screen.
